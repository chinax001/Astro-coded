Learn

The building blocks.

Each part of the chart answers a different question. Learn these, and the explorer above will make more sense.

01

Bhavas (houses)

Twelve areas of life: self, wealth, siblings, home, children, health, partnership, transformation, fortune, career, gains, and release.

02

Grahas (planets)

The Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu. Each one shows how its energy works in a house.

03

Drishti (aspects)

Every planet aspects the 7th house from itself. Mars adds the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, and Saturn the 3rd and 10th. Schools differ on Rahu and Ketu, so this tool uses 5th, 7th, and 9th.

04

Rashi and Lagna

Your Rashi is the Moon's sign at birth. Your Lagna is the sign rising at birth. Both need an exact birth time and place to calculate.

05

Nakshatra

The Moon's position among 27 star groups. It adds detail to personality and to the timing of events.

06

Dasha periods

Planetary periods that show which life themes are active over time. Always read them with the full chart.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my birth details to use the chart explorer?

No. The explorer is a learning tool. You place planets yourself to see how aspects work. Your personal reading is where your birth details matter.

Is the free basics tool a full Vedic chart?

Partly. With your birth time and place it shows a free Vedic chart: Lagna, Rashi, Nakshatra and planet houses. Dasha periods and a full interpretation come with your personal reading.

Why do I need my birth time?

Your Lagna changes roughly every two hours, so the birth time changes the chart. If you don't know it, tell us and the reading will say so.

How fast do I get my reading?

Within 24 hours after your payment is confirmed.

Is astrology scientific?

Astrology is a traditional practice used for reflection and guidance. It is not scientific evidence, so don't rely on it alone for medical, legal, or financial decisions.
